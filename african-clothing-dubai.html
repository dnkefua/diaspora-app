Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy African clothing in Dubai?

African-owned designers, tailors and fabric sellers operate across Deira (fabric souk), Bur Dubai, Al Karama, International City and JLT. The Diaspora App lists verified African fashion businesses across Dubai and the wider UAE with direct contact for fittings and orders.

Yes — many listed designers offer bespoke measurement, fittings and occasion wear, including Cameroonian Toghu, Nigerian aso-ebi, Senegalese boubou, Ghanaian kente and Ethiopian habesha kemis. Contact them directly to discuss timelines.

No. We never take a commission on sales. Designers pay only for visibility and premium placement.

How the Diaspora Score works

The score balances quality with depth, plus a small trust bonus to reward businesses that go through verification. It's intentionally transparent so any business can see what to improve.

  • Average rating · up to 60 points — your customers' actual experience, on a 5-star scale.
  • Review depth · up to 30 points — one review per point, capped at 30. A 4.9 with 30 reviews ranks above a 5.0 with 1.
  • Verified bonus · +5 points — earned through a physical site visit + records check.
  • Premium bonus · +10 points — for businesses that subscribe and pass quarterly re-inspection.

Maximum: 105 / 100. Time-window leaderboards (Week, Month, Year) only count reviews posted in that window. The All-time tab uses every review on the platform.

Frequently asked.

Do you really take zero commission?

Yes. Every order or booking goes directly between you and your customer. We charge for visibility tiers only. There are no transaction fees, no hidden cuts, no marketplace lock-in.

What does "Verified" actually require?

A Diaspora moderator visits your premises in person, confirms your trade licence, checks owner identity, and writes a short public visit report attached to your listing. Verification is awarded once — your blue tick stays as long as you stay in good standing. See the full criteria.

How do I pay?

Card or bank transfer in AED. Invoiced monthly or annually — annual gets 10% off. We're rolling out MOMO Pay (mobile money) and Botim integration next. Card payments are processed by a regulated payment provider; we never see your card details.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. No contracts. Cancel from your seller dashboard or email nkefua@ndnanalytics.com — we pro-rate any unused balance to the day.

What's the difference between Featured and Elite?

Featured is editorial visibility across the platform — homepage rotation, category rails, blog and social inclusion — at a self-serve price. Elite is a custom partnership with dedicated campaigns, hero placements, event tie-ins, and a direct line to a Diaspora editor. Elite is right when you want to own visibility in your market for a season or a launch.

Multi-city campaigns?

Featured + Elite tiers can run in multiple cities. Second city gets 15% off; three or more get 25% off. Talk to nkefua@ndnanalytics.com for a quote.

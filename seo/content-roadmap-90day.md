# 90‑Day SEO Content & Growth Roadmap — The Diaspora App

Goal: move the UAE African‑business cluster from *eligible* to *ranking & converting*. On‑page is largely done; the next 90 days are **indexing, authority (links), local signals, reviews, and depth**.

---

## Month 1 — Index, measure, and local foundations
**Technical / indexing (week 1)**
- [ ] Set up **Google Search Console** (DNS verification), submit `sitemap.xml`, request indexing for the 6 cluster URLs. *(See `search-console-checklist.md`.)*
- [ ] Confirm the cluster pages get **Indexed** in GSC; fix any "Crawled — not indexed".
- [ ] Re‑run **PageSpeed Insights** on the 5 landing pages + homepage; act on any CWV flags.

**Local & brand signals (the biggest lever for "near me" / Map pack)**
- [ ] Create a **Google Business Profile** for The Diaspora App; encourage each *verified* business to claim/keep its own GBP.
- [ ] Lock **NAP consistency** (name, contact, UAE) across site, GBP, LinkedIn, Instagram, app stores.

**Content (light)**
- [ ] 2 blog posts mapped to the cluster (internal‑link to verticals):
  - "Where to find African food in Dubai (neighbourhood guide)"
  - "A guide to Cameroonian dishes — fufu & eru, achu, ekwang, ndolé"

**Reviews**
- [ ] Seed genuine **customer reviews** on listed businesses (drives Best‑of‑Diaspora + trust signals). No fake reviews — ever.

## Month 2 — Authority (backlinks) + depth
**Backlinks (the #1 differentiator for head terms)**
- [ ] Get listed/linked from **UAE expat & African‑community** sites, Facebook/WhatsApp groups, event pages, embassy/cultural‑association pages.
- [ ] Pitch a **press/launch story** ("first curated directory of African businesses in the UAE — Cameroon‑first") to local/diaspora media.
- [ ] Partner cross‑links: each verified business links to its Diaspora App profile from its own site/socials.

**Content depth (only with real inventory)**
- [ ] Country cuisine pages as restaurant volume grows: "Nigerian restaurants Dubai", "Ethiopian restaurants Dubai".
- [ ] 2–3 more cluster blog posts: "African fashion designers in Dubai", "Where to buy African groceries in Dubai", "Best African hair salons & braiders in Dubai".
- [ ] Add `CollectionPage` JSON‑LD to `best.html` and `feed.html`.

**Measure**
- [ ] GSC monthly review: queries with impressions but low CTR → rewrite titles/descriptions; pages in positions 8–30 → expand content + add internal links.

## Month 3 — Expand the cluster + iterate
**Geographic expansion (avoid thin/doorway pages — only if listings exist)**
- [ ] Abu Dhabi variants: `african-restaurants-abu-dhabi.html`, etc., once there are real Abu Dhabi listings.
- [ ] Sharjah hub section if inventory supports it.

**Conversion + freshness**
- [ ] Add use‑case proof to vertical pages (photos, real reviews) as inventory grows.
- [ ] Keep `lastmod` accurate; refresh pages when listings change materially.
- [ ] Encourage ongoing reviews + new listings (freshness + UGC signal).

**Review & plan next quarter**
- [ ] GSC: which clusters are winning? Double down. Which are stuck? Diagnose (links? intent mismatch? thin?).
- [ ] Decide on additional verticals (events, churches/faith, real estate/bed‑space) based on demand.

---

## Guardrails (from Google + the skill)
- People‑first content; **no** keyword stuffing, doorway pages, scaled thin AI pages, fake reviews/ratings/locations, or schema for invisible content.
- One intent per page; build topical authority with genuinely useful supporting content.
- Report progress honestly — no guaranteed‑#1 claims.

## What success looks like (realistic)
- **Weeks:** long‑tail Cameroon‑first terms start ranking & getting impressions.
- **1–3 months:** mid‑tail terms ("African grocery Dubai", "African braiding Dubai") move into striking distance with links + reviews.
- **3–6+ months:** competitive head terms ("African restaurants Dubai") improve as domain authority and local signals accrue.

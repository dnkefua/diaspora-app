How verification works

Apply

Email nkefua@ndnanalytics.com from your business email with your listing URL, trade licence number, and best contact for a site visit.

Schedule

A city editor reaches out within 5 business days to schedule a 30-60 minute on-site visit. You'll get a checklist of records to have ready.

Visit

The editor inspects the premises, verifies records, takes a few photos for our archive, and writes a short visit report.

Verdict

If you pass, your listing receives the blue tick within 48 hours. If we can't verify yet, we'll send specific feedback so you can fix and re-apply.

What we check

  • The premises exist and match the listing. Address, hours, signage.
  • Trade licence / business registration. Active and in the owner's name (or a director's where applicable).
  • Owner identity. Photo ID matched against the registration.
  • Category-specific credentials. Restaurants: hygiene rating + halal / kosher cert if claimed. Salons / barbers: cosmetology licences if required by jurisdiction. Photographers: portfolio + commercial use rights. Tailors: physical workshop or atelier.
  • Customer-facing safety. Visible fire exits, first-aid, sanitation.
  • Owner is reachable. Working WhatsApp / phone / email.
  • Diaspora connection. The business is owned, operated, or built around the global diaspora — that's our entire point.

How Premium works

Premium is the highest trust tier. To qualify you must:

  1. Be Verified (blue tick) first.
  2. Pay the Premium subscription (monthly or annual; your city editor will share rates for your market).
  3. Pass a short re-inspection — we want to confirm the premises, records, and credentials remain in good standing before the gold mark goes live.

Premium businesses are re-inspected quarterly. If conditions slip materially, we revert to Verified or remove the badge entirely. We refund any unused balance pro-rated to the day.

What badges do not mean

  • Verified is not a guarantee. It's a snapshot of due diligence on the day we visited.
  • Premium is not pay-to-win. You cannot buy Premium without first being Verified, and you cannot stay Premium without passing re-inspections.
  • Sponsored placements (Featured Spotlight, On the Menu, In the Chair) are advertising slots — they do not affect verification status. A Sponsored ad from an unverified business will never show a blue tick.
